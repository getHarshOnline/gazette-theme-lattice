This repository owns Lattice’s presentation. `design-tokens` owns values and the workspace supplies identity, content, selected sources and destinations. Follow the [build and check route](README.md#build-and-check); edit values at the producer, not in generated captures.

Preserve the seven-part continuous field and its dark ground in both modes; interiors retain their own light and dark surfaces. The field is illustrative. Boundary, alternatives, controlled comparison and ordered process are different relationships; choose the form from the supplied meaning.

Check recognition without accent and held light in both modes. Fixtures use fictional content; presentation preserves facts, units, sources, status and destinations. Report engine gaps to Base or Gazette’s owner while retaining the approved treatment. Implementation and checks do not constitute approval. Work in this repository; the workspace chooses what renders.
